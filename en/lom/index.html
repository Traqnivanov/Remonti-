What foreign buyers ask

Do you work in Lom, Bulgaria? +

Yes. I'm based in Lom and cover the entire Montana region — Berkovitsa, Kozloduy and surrounding villages. Free inspection visit included.

Can I manage the renovation without being on-site? +

Yes. I send daily photos and videos via WhatsApp so you can follow every stage from anywhere in the world. Many of my clients manage everything remotely.

Do you accept Revolut or card payment? +

I accept Revolut, SEPA bank transfer, or cash in EUR or BGN — whichever is most convenient for you.

Will the price change during the work? +

No. The price we agree after the inspection is final. It only changes if you decide to add new work yourself.

When do I pay? +

The price and payment terms are agreed in writing before work starts. Materials are paid upfront. Labour is paid in agreed stages or upon completion of each stage. The total price does not change unless you add new work.

Is there a written guarantee? +

Yes. Every service comes with a signed written guarantee. If anything is not right, I come back and fix it at no extra cost.

Do you speak English? +

We stay in touch via WhatsApp, Viber, Messenger or email. Daily photos and videos make almost everything clear. When something needs to be clarified, we always find a way.

Who buys the materials? +

Your choice. I can source the materials with a detailed list, or you can buy them yourself. We agree on everything before work starts — quantities, quality level, and budget.

Do I need to be in Bulgaria during the renovation? +

No. Many of my clients manage everything from abroad. Daily photos and videos via WhatsApp, Viber or Messenger keep you fully informed at every stage. You only need to be here for the final handover — or we can do that by video call too.

Can you source the materials on my behalf? +

Yes. I can prepare a detailed materials list and handle the purchasing locally. You benefit from local knowledge and supplier contacts without needing to organise anything from abroad.

Do you work with inherited properties? +

Yes. I regularly work on older inherited houses and apartments in Lom and the Montana region. I can assess the property, advise on priorities, and carry out the renovation to a agreed budget.
